      About this project

      Detecting Modern LLM-Generated Fake News: A Cross-Generator Robustness Study.

      Five detectors (Logistic Regression, Linear SVM, SGD-Huber, Complement NB on TF-IDF word 1–2-grams, and Gradient Boosting on stylometric features) were trained on human news, then stress-tested with noise, perturbations, short texts, and text from several LLM generators.

      Limitations

      • Short snippets (under ~80 words) are less reliable.
      • Models learn patterns of the training data; a verdict is a statistical estimate, not proof.
      • Source-specific giveaway tokens (e.g. agency names, URLs) are removed before classification.
      • Do not use as the sole basis for judging whether a story is true.
